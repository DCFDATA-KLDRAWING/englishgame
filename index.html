Review 1 核心文法對比與擂台

Unit 1: 地點/國籍 (用 Be 動詞)
  • • Where are you from? (不可用 do)
  • • She is from Japan. (不可用 has)
  • • 國名首字母大寫：Taiwan, Canada
Unit 2: 身體不適 (用 have / has)
  • • What's the matter? (問病情)
  • • I have a cold. (感冒)
  • • He has a fever. (三單主詞用 has)
